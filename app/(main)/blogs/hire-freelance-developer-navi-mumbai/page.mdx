# How to Hire a Freelance Developer in Navi Mumbai: 12 Questions to Ask

_Published on November 22, 2026 • 5 min read_

**Short answer:** to hire a good freelance developer in Navi Mumbai, check live work, ask who owns the code and domain, get a written scope and price, and talk to the person who will write the code. Below are the twelve questions I would ask.

I’m Rushabh Bhosale, a freelance full stack developer in Navi Mumbai. I’d want these answers if I were hiring.

## Freelancer, agency or in-house?

- **Freelancer:** you work directly with the person building, usually at lower cost. Capacity is limited to one person.
- **Agency:** more people and process, with higher cost and an account manager between you and the code.
- **In-house hire:** best if you have continuous work for months, at the cost of salary and management.

For a website, store, app or MVP, a freelancer is often the quickest route to a start.

## 12 questions to ask

1. **Can I see live work?** Open the sites and apps yourself. See my [projects](/work).
2. **Which parts did you build?** Ask what the developer personally did.
3. **Will you give me a written scope and price?** Do not start without one.
4. **What is not included?** Domain, hosting, content and third-party fees are common extras.
5. **Who owns the code, domain and accounts?** They should be in your name.
6. **How long will it take, and what do you need from me?** Delays usually come from missing content.
7. **How will we communicate?** Ask for a regular update rhythm and a preview link.
8. **How do payments work?** Staged payments tied to milestones protect both sides.
9. **How will my site be found on Google?** Ask about titles, sitemap, speed and structured data.
10. **What happens after launch?** Ask about bug fixes and support.
11. **Can we meet?** In Navi Mumbai and Mumbai, many developers meet in person; others use video calls.
12. **Can I contact a past client?** References are a good test.

## Warning signs

- A price with no written scope.
- Refusing to share examples of previous work.
- Keeping the domain, hosting or code in their name.
- Guaranteeing Google rankings or AI recommendations. Nobody honestly can.
