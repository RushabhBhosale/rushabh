# How Much Does a Mobile App Cost in India? Android and iOS Price Guide

_Published on November 22, 2026 • 6 min read_

**Short answer:** a cross-platform React Native app for Android and iOS starts from {inr(price("mobile-app"))} with me, typically taking four to ten weeks. The final cost depends on the number of screens, backend work and integrations.

I’m Rushabh Bhosale, a developer in Navi Mumbai who builds React Native apps professionally at Peracto Infotech and independently. Here is how app pricing works.

## One codebase or two?

Building a separate native Android app and iOS app means writing everything twice. **React Native** lets me build one codebase that runs on both, which lowers cost and keeps features in step. When the platform needs it, I write native code in Kotlin or Java, as I did for a Bluetooth health-device app and an Android SMS reader.

## What drives the price of an app

- **Number of screens.** Each screen needs design, build and testing.
- **Backend.** Most apps need an API, a database and user accounts.
- **Integrations.** Payments (Razorpay), maps, video calls, push notifications and Bluetooth each add work.
- **Design.** A custom visual design costs more than a standard layout.
- **Store release.** Developer accounts, listings, screenshots and review rounds take time.

## Costs beyond development

- Google Play developer account (one-time fee) and Apple Developer Program (yearly fee), both paid to the platforms.
- Servers or cloud services for the backend.
- Third-party service fees, such as video calling or SMS.
